Birthday Party Stream

Preparing for the Birthday Party Stream

This shortcake is super tasty. Thank you, Shinobu-kun…♪

Nishishi, I’m so glad to see you happy with it~♪

Thanks for giving me a slice, too~♪ But you sure it was okay not to eat this during the stream?

Ah, you’re right. Maybe it was a waste to eat it right now…?

But as long as we’re chatting with you, we should be able to have exciting talks no matter what. We might come to find it was better we ate it now than later.

You’re right… Okay, since there’s no more cake to eat for the steam, let’s talk lots when it starts~♪

Start of the Birthday Party Stream

“Ermm… Thank you all for coming to my birthday stream today.”

“I’m sorry I took a while to start the stream, I was having a bit of trouble with the preparations.”

“Woah…! There’s so many comments saying, ‘don’t worry about it!’…”

“Thank you so much. I’m so glad all my fans are so kind…♪”

“I’ll do my best to get this stream moving smoothly, so please watch over me.”

“I’m making my stream guests wait, so let’s get started…♪”

During the Birthday Party Stream 1

“The first guest is Ra*bits’ Mashiro-kun.”

“Thanks for inviting me as a guest, I’m happy to be here! ♪”

“I have very few friends I can casually invite to things, so you saved me by accepting the offer…”

“C’mon, don’t say something so sad! There were so many people who wanted to celebrate your birthday, like Mitsuru and Hinata, y’know?”

“Alright, here. On behalf of everyone, I’m gifting you yuru-chara stationery~♪”

“S-So cute…! I didn’t think I’d get something this amazing for my birthday, thank you…♪”

During the Birthday Party Stream 2

“The second guest is Shinobu-kun. Thanks for coming here…♪”

“You are most welcome! Let me say it again, happy birthday~♪”

“I got shortcake from Shinobu-kun. But it was so tasty that I ended up eating it way before the stream started.”

“Perhaps I gave it to you a little too early? But still, it made me so happy to see you gladly eat it~♪”

“Your kindness really seeps into my heart… We took photos, so let me show them to all you fans out there.”

During the Birthday Party Stream 3

“It looks like it’s about time we end the stream… since I started a bit late, I prolonged the time a bit, but how was it overall?”

“Erm… ‘I’m so glad I got to hear Midori-kun talk a lot, it’s pretty rare to see that’?”

“Ahaha, I’m always trying not to stand out much, that’s why. And maybe it’s also because there’s a pretty loud person in my unit…?”

“The point is, I’m glad to hear this stream turned out fun for you all…♪”

“I’ll keep on doing my best with all my fellow Ryuseitai unitmates, so I would be happy to get your support.”
