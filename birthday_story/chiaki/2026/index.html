“That’s just like ya, huh? Sounds good to me. Break a leg out there without goin’ too far.”

“Yeah! I’ll keep firing myself up even further as a dependable hero! ☆”

“yes, okay. i want chiaki to continue being [energetic] forevermore, after all…♪”
