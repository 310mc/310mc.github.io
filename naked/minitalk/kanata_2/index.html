those [marshmallows] have such an adorable [shape] to them, don’t they?

are they shaped like the [yuru-charas] that have been trending…?

i knew it~ i am so glad i got it [right]~♪

How did you know?

when i went [shopping] with kaoru, i spotted them at a [shop].

that was when kaoru told me that they are apparently trending right now.

and the [character] you are eating is the most popular one…♪

Do you like them?

no, the one who likes these [characters] is midori.

he was collecting their [key chains], so i asked him what sort of [characters] they are.

2/3

midori loves [yuru-chara], so whenever i catch [sight] of them, i get a little curious.1 i sometimes also look them up.

but it is hard to remember all their [names].

so i always end up asking midori about them in the end.

A way to learn their names would be…

mm? you will teach me the [trick] to remembering their names?

oh, so it is easier to remember if i associate each one with its [special characteristic].

i see, i will try it out. i will let you know if it [worked]…♪

It’s hard to remember, right?

yes. there are a lot of [yuru-chara] with similar-sounding [names], after all…

but you know, midori can name every single one of them if you ask him.

so anzu-san, if you are ever stuck because you cannot tell them apart, i think it is worth asking him for help…♪

3/3

whenever i [stream], all our [fans] tell me about the current [trends].

they even teach me all about the trendy [characters], for example.

so i have been [studying] up a lot thanks to that…♪

That sounds like a nice live stream.

yes. i get to [study] and [communicate] with our [fans] at the same time.

next time, i will ask them to teach me about popular [food].

i am excited to find out what has been trending lately~♪

I get to study up on things, as well.

you too, anzu-san? what do you mean…?

ah, you mean that you watch [live streams] from different people to [learn] from them.

in that case, i will talk about all my [fish knowledge] the next time i [stream]~♪
