Naked – Chiaki Mini Talk: Cooking Made With Love!

Officially released on August 15, 2026 Enstars , 4. Character , Chiaki
2.3k ~3 min
Mini Talk
Point of View
Anzu
Translation
310mc
Story
Naked

1/3

Lately, everyone in Ryuseitai has been busy with their personal work, right? I’m sure exhaustion is starting to build up inside them.

That’s why I’d like to cook them something that could energize their spirits!1

Mm~ I’m not sure what to make them exactly…

That sounds great.

Ooh, you think so!?

To be frank, I was actually worried everyone would tell me that they don’t need that…

But if you approve of my idea, then I should be able to push through with confidence~♪

You’re busy too, aren’t you, Senpai?

Yeah, I am. But that doesn’t act as an excuse not to cheer them up.

We’re a unit, after all. It’s important that we join arms for each other’s sake no matter the time and place, right?

I’d love to make something that bursts them with energy with just one fill…!

2/3

Now that I’ve decided to make them food, the question is what to make…

Should I go with grilled fish or rolled omelette?2 Or maybe…

Anzu, what kind of food do you think they’d find simple to eat while having a busy life?

How about onigiri?

Ohh, I see! That might be a really good idea.

With onigiri, you can nourish yourself with healthy food with just one hand, even when you’re busy with work.

They won’t get bored of the taste if I make a variety of them with different ingredients, and there’s the excitement of choosing whichever you like. Alright, I’ll go with that!

How about miso soup?

Miso soup, huh… I didn’t think of that; it does sound good.

And it’d be abundant with protein if I add in regular or fried tofu, so it’d be good for their nutrition.

The best part is, it should be able to soothe their exhausted bodies. Alright, miso soup it is!

3/3

That all being said, if I’m making enough food for everyone in Ryuseitai, it’s going to be a lot…

Even shopping for ingredients will be demanding on me.

Anzu, if it’s alright with you, could you help me out with cooking…?

Of course.

And there it is, with her immediate reply! Thank you, Anzu~♪

Alright then, let’s start by shopping first. Are you ready to head on out?

With you around, I should be able to decide on which ingredients to buy in a snap! ☆

I was just about to offer.

You were actually just about to offer helping me…?

Ahh, you truly are so kind, Anzu. I’m a little worried you might exhaust yourself from helping us so much, honestly.

But this time around, you’ll be saving me from so much trouble. I’d really appreciate your help with this!

Translation Notes

  1. ↑ Chiaki picked up cooking, first shown in I’m At Your Service, Master!, briefly shown in Banquet Beneath the Clouded Moon, and explored further in VS★GOURMET. This event story also has him cooking in the chapters!
  2. ↑ Chiaki has cooked these before; both meals in I’m At Your Service, Master!, and grilled fish in Banquet Beneath the Clouded Moon (Chapter 1).
