2wink VS Ryuseitai – Midori Mini Talk: Soothing Item

Officially released on October 31, 2026 Enstars , 4. Character , Midori
2k ~2 min
Mini Talk
Point of View
Anzu
Translation
310mc
Story
2wink VS Ryuseitai

1/3

Ahh, Anzu-san. Good to see you.

Me? I was just shopping for my Australia trip.

But then I realized MM is getting closer by the second, and started feeling depressed… Haah…

You’re not excited for it?

I wish I could be excited for the chance to be up on a huge stage, but…

I can’t help but feel the pressure when I think about being up on the limelight with the whole world watching.

Ahh, just talking about it made me even more depressed…

Take deep breaths.

Taking deep breaths will help relax me?

…Wheww. I think I feel a little more relaxed now.

Well, not that this changes the fact that I’ll have to appear on MM or anything, though.

2/3

Actually, if there’s something out there that could soothe me from the nerves, I might want it…

I guess that’d be yuru-chara merch. Or maybe something that gives off a nice fragrance…

Anzu-san, could you share with me any suggestions, if you have any?

A squishy, maybe?

Oh yeah, that would feel nice to touch, so it could be soothing.

Huh? There’s a sloppy-shaped, cute squishy trending right now?

I’d love to know more! Something that feels nice to touch and cute would be perfect…♪

How about aroma oil?

Oh yeah, I might be able to relax with some aroma oil.

But there’s a lot of fragrances out there, so I don’t think I’ll know which to pick…

You’ll help me out, Anzu-san? Then I’d love to take up that offer, if it’s okay…♪

3/3

Phew, I’m so glad I got to buy me something that could soothe me.

Plus, if anyone in Ryuseitai ever feels nervous, I could lend it to them.

…Actually, never mind. I bet I’m the only one who gets this stressed among Ryuseitai…

But still…

Even if they didn’t feel nervous, they’d be happy to know I’d be willing to lend it to them?

I wonder… Well, I’m sure they wouldn’t be mean about it, though…

…Alright, I’ll lend it to them if I ever feel like it.

Even if they look calm on the outside…

Even if they look calm, they might actually be feeling the pressure?

You might be right about that… We’re going up on a huge stage, so there’s no way they aren’t feeling a thing.

I’ll keep an eye on everyone for now… and I notice they need something to soothe them, I’ll lend this to them.
