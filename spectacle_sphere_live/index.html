Tweet Feed

During the Fan ThanX Festival event, the unit in charge of the festival takes over the twitter account for around 2 weeks. After that, they will sparsely post tweets, typically when a non-Fan ThanX event is occurring (ex. Tour event or Shuffle).

This is a translation of all the tweets posted by Ryuseitai, or the tweets related to Ryuseitai.

※ As of August 27 2026, the twitter account was rebranded to be for the Spectacle Sphere Live. This tweet feed retains the layout it was in during the Fan ThanX Festival period.
